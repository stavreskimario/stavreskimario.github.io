# P5 finish study: independent primary-photo review

The broad continuous stringer cheeks, horizontal landing band and exposed gallery fascia justify a bounded flat-face **appearance study**. The photographs do not establish an 8 mm cover-sheet construction or the proposed seam positions. This is source eligibility for a prototype, not acceptance of a finished historical stair.

| Feature | Source reading and acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Stringer and landing side faces | Broad continuous visible faces occur in both LOC crops. Review the complete two-flight stair in an oblique side view; preserve open track views. |
| Landing haunches | The sloping lower stringer outline eases into the horizontal landing with visible curves. A straight-panel-only result leaves this silhouette incomplete. Exact radii remain inferred. |
| Rails and returns | Smooth direction changes and returned/terminal forms are visible. Retaining existing rails preserves contacts but does not verify their shape; inspect their curves independently. |
| Newels and feet | Ball finials, shaped shafts and visible base/collar forms appear. The buried pad depth is unknown; the photographs do not justify raising anchors by 207.5 mm or inventing decorative feet. |
| Gallery edge | Keep the lower Exit edge distinct from the prominent rounded upper-concourse fascia. A profile from one level must not be assigned to the other without registered evidence. |
| Guard panels | Vertical divisions, diagonal motifs and circular junction details have qualitative support. Exact counts, diameters and ornament profiles still require comparison. |

Neither LOC crop identifies P5. Describe this as a **representative arrival-stair family comparison**, not an authenticated P5 camera match. The opening-era photographs do not verify the complete 1935–1940 condition, colours, alloy or fabrication schedule. Physical contact and component-preservation checks remain independent gates.

Before repeating the prototype, inspect (1) the whole two-flight side silhouette, (2) the curved stringer/landing and handrail junctions, and (3) the lower gallery edge in a wider view that identifies both deck levels. Missing curves should remain an explicit geometry stage. No candidate render or model has been inspected in this review.

The [LOC item](https://www.loc.gov/item/2016815392/) and [ASCE plate LXXXIV Fig.2](https://archive.org/download/transactionsofam69amer/transactionsofam69amer.pdf#page=439) identify the existing primary-reference context.
