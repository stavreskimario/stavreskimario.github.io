# Tread-count and curvature addendum

The raw source independently contains **400 upward horizontal tread surfaces in 32 flight meshes**: 13 + 12 internal goings per stair, or 25. The generator uses 14 + 13 risers, or 27; gallery, intermediate landing and platform terminal surfaces are separate. A photographic front-edge count therefore cannot be compared directly with “25 treads” without matching conventions.

| Cached crop / lower arrival-stair segment | Conservatively traced visible interior edges | Limit |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Left detail, central stair’s lower flight | At least 11 | Selected open interior segment only; foreground steel and endpoint surfaces prevent a complete authenticated count. |
| Right detail, central stair’s upper flight | At least 7 | Clear interior edges only; lower newel/rails obscure more candidates and the terminal deck edge is excluded. |

The other flight in each crop is obscured by steelwork, the concrete pier, guards or the crop boundary. Numbered inspection figures identify the selected source-pixel locations. **These are partial lower bounds, not complete flight counts; do not sum different photographed stairs or fill hidden steps.** They neither confirm nor demonstrate a contradiction with the authored 25-goings/27-risers family. The full historical count remains a plan/registered-photo review hold before repeating it as verified. Neither crop identifies P5.

The directly supported curve is the **visible lower stringer/landing outline**, which eases from the rising diagonal into the horizontal landing band with a fuller rounded haunch beneath the meeting. The photographs do not expose a structural centreline or establish whether this was a bent member, varying-depth haunch, cover or trim. The opposite edge is partly obscured. Thus the images support an outer-profile study while preserving tread seats and host centres; they do not justify bending the whole structural member or selecting an exact radius. Rail transitions require their own curve trace.

`geometry-tread-census.json` under `tread-inspection/` records the independent raw triangle/height census. `tread-curvature-addendum.json` retains counts, coordinate files, source/geometry hashes and all limits. The marked crops are analysis annotations; original source files remain unchanged. No model, GPU, root/runtime or publication changes were made.
