# Post-bake bounded acceptance

1. Pin the completed packet, rawHDR/AO/report and repairedGLB identities. Confirm preserve-mode imported-buffer and selected-only-active guards, unchanged source visibility for all seven contexts, and the actual new AO image in all11 occlusion slots.
2. Compare repaired raw against the UV-only input: geometry, indices, normal vectors and UV0 remain physically equivalent; UV1 must retain the new authored coordinates. AO repair itself is a stronger byte-prefix/JSON proof and needs no geometric tolerance. Fresh exported normal re-encoding, if any, is measured explicitly.
3. Recompute physical UV1 density on the actual baked/encoded data. Confirm new public column/vault/wall/floor budgets and the whole-chart affine/continuity checks. Optimizers may renumber atlas channels; follow actual occlusion texture texCoord and verify the runtime channel rather than assumeUV1.
4. Audit every material role through raw→resize→compression: albedo, normal and MR source identity, transfer/packing, dimensions, channel variance and scalar strength. A normal image becoming flat before compression is not fixed by higher compression quality later. Keep that audit separate from geometry preservation.
5. At matching real browser viewpoints compare the same exposure/light/quality: Ionic capital/shaft, side wall, inner vault and floor contact. Review raw vs denoised mapped light only if diagnosing filtering;256samples does not substitute for spatial atlas density. Confirm no new old-AO pattern, island-edge smear or contact instability.
6. Preserve prior physical routes and source qualifications. Do not claim the lowLOD or historic mural acquires new authenticated detail. No broad architectural re-audit is needed when unchanged-buffer ownership passes.
