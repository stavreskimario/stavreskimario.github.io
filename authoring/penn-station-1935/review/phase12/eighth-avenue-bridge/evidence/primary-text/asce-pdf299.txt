The  site  of  the  viaduct  was  excavated  to  sub-grade,  a  timber  trestle 
was  erected  to  maintain  the  highway  for  traffic,  and  the  north  and 
south   abutments  were  built  under  another  contract. 
Some  interesting  incidents  of  the  construction  of  this  viaduct  are 
the  following:  Top  of  rock  was  found  below  track  level  over  the 
greater  portion  of  the  south  half  of  the  viaduct,  gradually  rising  to 
the  surface  of  the  avenue  in  the  north  half.  Pockets  of  disintegrated 
feldspar,  having  the  consistency  of  thoroughly  compacted  ashes,  and 
saponifying  in  water,  were  encountered  in  several  instances  imme- 
diately beneath  the  viaduct  foundations  of  the  six  southerly  bents.
