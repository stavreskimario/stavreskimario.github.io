# Pennsylvania Station — architectural study, revision 1

This is a **phased reconstruction**, not a surveyed replica or a finished photoreal visualisation. The first delivery establishes an evidence-led exterior, an explorable main waiting room, and a lower-confidence interpretation of the glass concourse. It deliberately does not claim that the surrounding street blocks, furnishings, steel member profiles, or every decorative carving reproduce 1935 conditions.

## Authoring and reproduction

`build_station.py` runs inside the official Blender 4.3 `bpy` module. The .blend source preserves Geometry Nodes authoring groups for the repeated Seventh Avenue and side Doric colonnades. The portable GLBs contain the evaluated geometry and use glTF 2.0 physically based materials. The model is not generated by browser JavaScript.

From this application's parent repository, with `uv` available:

```sh
uv run --no-project --python 3.11 --with bpy==4.3.0 --with 'numpy<2' python penn-station-1935/model/build_station.py
```

This writes three separate GLBs, a compressed editable `.blend`, nine original 1024×1024 stone texture maps, and `scene-manifest.json`. Runtime optimisation is a separate step; keep the source GLBs and source script reproducible when optimising derived assets. Do not label raw authoring output KTX2/Meshopt compressed unless those transformations have actually run.

All coordinates are in metres. Blender X points east toward Seventh Avenue, Y north toward West33rd Street and Z up. glTF X points east, Y up and Z south. The ground is at0; grade differences and accurate inter-room stair elevations are an explicit future phase. The three chunks are already placed in the same coordinate frame.

## Primary evidence inspected

1. **McKim, Mead & White monograph, volume IV (1915), plates300–310.** [Public-domain scan, Getty Research Institute/Internet Archive](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/b/b3/A_monograph_of_the_work_of_McKim%2C_Mead_%26_White%2C_1879-1915_.._%28IA_gri_33125006526038%29.pdf). Plate300 gives the circulation plan;301 the Seventh Avenue elevation;306 the waiting room photograph;308 its interior order;309 the exterior Doric order and balustrade. These plates were visually reviewed before modelling. The lower-confidence concourse interpretation also uses the architect monograph's concourse photograph.
2. **Pennsylvania Railroad, Pennsylvania Station in New York City (1909).** [Library of Congress scan](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/public/gdcmassbookdig/pennsylvaniastat00penn/pennsylvaniastat00penn.pdf). The nominal784×430ft footprint,277×103ft waiting room,150ft floor-to-roof, and225×45ft arcade provide numerical anchors. The340×210ft figure is expressly for the concourse **with adjacent courtyard**, and has not been applied as the enclosed roof dimensions.
3. **Pennsylvania Railroad, Pennsylvania Station (1910).** Contemporary descriptions give the35ft exterior column height,4ft6in diameter and the7ft clock centred61ft above pavement. The same descriptions differ in their outside building dimensions; see the uncertainty ledger below.
4. **NYC Municipal Archives.** [Re-discovering the Old Pennsylvania Station](https://www.archives.nyc/blog/2024/6/7/re-discovering-the-old-pennsylvania-station), original1906 approved elevations and1936 photographs. The target period is1935–1940, but the base geometry follows original plans; a complete alterations audit for that period has not been completed.

Historical scans were used as evidence. The nine material maps are new, deterministic procedural work created for this model and do not contain sampled historical photographs. The `.blend`, generated geometry and original textures are project-created assets.

## Reconstruction ledger and limitations

- The outside footprint appears as both784×430ft and788ft9in×430ft6in in period publications. Revision1 uses the first, explicitly nominal figure. Reconcile the measured approved plan before claiming an exact outside-to-outside replica.
- Main-room placement is read from plate300 and rounded to the common model origin. It is not a survey registration. The precise clear-room versus314ft4in×108ft8in overall shell distinction needs a measured elevation/section reconciliation.
- Coffer spacing, capital leaves, moulding depths, ashlar seams and baluster contours are interpretations. The Corinthian capitals have abstracted acanthus relief, not scans of surviving fragments. Some secondary facade bay spacing is simplified and has not been individually dimensioned.
- The waiting room's end screens, ticket grilles, benches, lamp standards and stair counts establish the spatial experience; exact furnishing arrangement in1935 has not been established. The clock is fixed to an illustrative time.
- The concourse uses intersecting glazed roof bays, visible iron piers, truss depth and glazing bars. Member sections, roof curvature, pier positions and the five illustrative platform-access wells require detailed engineering-plan calibration. Its59×98m clear envelope is a **plan-derived interpretation**, not an authenticated dimension. No complete track level or period rolling stock is included.
- Street/context geometry gives the station an urban frame, but surrounding buildings are unsigned low-rise massing, not independently authenticated Manhattan properties. Traffic, pedestrians, signage and a parcel-by-parcel1930s map reconstruction remain future work. Do not interpret the visible context as a verified1935 streetscape.
- Surface colours and original PBR maps are plausible approximations. They have not been colour-calibrated to surviving Milford pink granite, travertine or Tennessee marble samples. No full lighting bake or historic weather/atmosphere reconstruction is claimed.
- Uniform ground elevation and illustrative stairs mean this is not a faithful circulation/accessibility survey. The browser should use room-specific navigation constraints and label the phase accurately.

## Subsequent acceptance gates

1. Register approved plans/elevations to one consistent exterior datum; reconcile published dimensions, room levels, every facade bay and colonnade count.
2. Replace abstracted capitals, sculpture, eagle/clock surrounds and decorative profiles with evidence-led high-resolution authoring; audit ticketing and furnishings for1935–1940.
3. Rebuild the concourse from structural sections and glazed roof plans, accurately cut stair openings, and model platforms/period PRR rolling stock.
4. Survey historical Manhattan parcels and street standards from maps and dated photographs; replace context massing rather than silently treating it as verified.
5. Bake physically calibrated indirect illumination, validate colour/exposure, prepare desktop/mobile LODs and compress textures/meshes; measure real browsers and physical phones.
6. Make matched-view archival/render comparison sheets, recording camera calibration and visible discrepancies. Only describe the result as photoreal or1:1 if those comparisons and dimensional checks support the claim.
