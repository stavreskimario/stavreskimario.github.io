# Asset provenance — material and interior checkpoint

## Newly authored assets

The architectural, neighbourhood, vehicle and rolling-stock meshes, Blender authoring scripts, procedural surface maps, lighting atlases and synthesized ambience were created for this project. Geometry interprets historical evidence; it is not a scan of the demolished station. Names such as Milford granite, travertine and Tuscan red describe the intended appearance without certifying a sampled historic material or paint colour.

Editable `.blend` files, original material maps, HDR bakes and AO PNGs are retained under repository-root `authoring/penn-station-1935/`. The custom staging process excludes that folder; the existing legacy root Pages deployment can serve it, so its entire contents are public-release material. Runtime Meshopt GLBs use lossless `.glb.gz` transport with browser decompression, requiring no backend or special response headers. The decompressed GLBs are byte-identical to the reviewed originals; geometry, material data and texture pixels are unchanged by this transport step. Shared KTX2 surface/AO textures and lossless WebP illumination atlases are served locally from `assets/`. Compression, map resizing and mechanical reference-image cropping do not introduce new historical evidence.

The [station ledger](model/README.md), [context ledger](model/context-research.md), [rolling-stock ledger](model/rolling-stock-research.md) and [research register](RESEARCH.md) identify the evidence and uncertain details. Generic 1930s-style cars and lamps are authored interpretations, not reproductions of an authenticated brand, exact street inventory or third-party 3D model.

## Lighting and environment

The four interpreted MilfordFine, InteriorFine, RomanTravertine and HonedMarble colour profiles and restrained normal strengths are retained from Phase 4. Their algorithms, palettes and nine unchanged CC0 source maps remain recorded under `authoring/penn-station-1935/materials/phase4/`. This checkpoint adds the selected B roughness derivatives documented in [roughness-provenance.json](../authoring/penn-station-1935/materials/phase5/roughness-provenance.json): InteriorGranite 0.30–0.48, MilfordGranite 0.55–0.78 and Travertine 0.60–0.78, each with roughnessFactor 1. The generator clips the original linear scalar at its recorded robust percentiles, maps it into the selected range and rounds to an 8-bit value. The source files and 2K/512px derivatives retain recorded hashes; the runtime uses Non-Color KTX2 data. These bounded finishes replace the earlier multipliers rather than multiplying the already dark maps again. Marble-family roughness, colour maps, surface UVs and normals are unchanged. No historic roughness, alloy or polish sample was measured.

The separate seamless Bedford-limestone micro-surface is newly authored by [prepare_bedford_surface.py](https://github.com/stavreskimario/stavreskimario.github.io/blob/d6d6cb169c2c109b08da6b956bf8e439e94ab8f6/penn-station-1935/model/prepare_bedford_surface.py), using a seeded periodic grain field rather than a photograph or scan. Its [separate provenance](../authoring/penn-station-1935/materials/phase4/bedford-surface-provenance.json) records the source-script hash, seed, 2K colour/roughness/OpenGL-normal files, tile size and measured map statistics. Bedford material identity follows the primary construction record; exact grain, colour, roughness and optical response remain interpretations. The maps contain their target mean colour and use a unit base colour factor, avoiding a second tint.

`OpalCandelabraGlass` is a separate authored material for the photographed five-globe standards. Its restrained milky response can be adjusted independently of clock-face and sign whites. The original glass composition, lamp output and photometry are uncalibrated.

This material/fixture checkpoint retains all six Phase 4 illumination atlases and AO bindings, plus the existing static probes. It does not deliver the pending 25-degree ceiling UV rebuild or fresh vault bake. The retained Phase 4 global-illumination pass uses **128 Metal samples and six diffuse bounces**, with **512 samples for the detailed order**. Schema 3 separates the waiting room into **8192px vault**, **4096px masonry** and **4096px order** atlases; exterior, concourse and platform use **2048px**. Desktop order AO remains at **4096px**. Final inputs include the repaired leaf shells and broad revision 3 prism court, with exact input hashes in each bake record. Fine concrete webs and ironwork stay outside the large masonry atlas. Directional sun remains dynamic; existing 512px derivatives provide the lighter fallback.

Source radiance and per-zone records are retained under `authoring/penn-station-1935/lighting/phase4/originals/`; filtered outputs are under `phase4/denoised/`. Full source and denoised HDR bytes are losslessly gzip archived with decoded SHA verification. Color-only OpenImageDenoise filters explicit linear radiance, followed by one sRGB encoding and normalized lossless WebP. The browser restores the scale and irradiance conversion. Each atlas records its observed 0.18 transfer control, clipping and source/output hashes. These controls and source settings do not by themselves establish final photographic fidelity.

The four retained Phase 4 waiting-room, concourse, platform and elevated Seventh Avenue captures use **2048×1024 HDR at 64 Metal samples**. Low **512×256** derivatives use Non-Color resizing and zero dither. Runtime room probes use lossless `.hdr.gz`; `assets/sky.hdr` is the full exterior capture. The delivered capture records identify the actual repaired geometry and interpreted material library. Static-shadow caching and front-face rendering of the closed floor prisms retain full glass transmission and refraction resolution; thin window glazing remains double-sided.

These static environments approximate the authored architecture and limited surroundings. They are not historical panoramas, complete 1935 streetscapes or reflections with positional parallax, moving passengers or time-specific indirect relighting. Daylight presets, material colour, original surface polish, lamp intensity, sky and exposure remain interpretations rather than calibrated historical measurements.

Earlier Phase 3 sources and reports remain preserved. The superseded **Spruit Sunrise** by Greg Zaal, from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/spruit_sunrise), remains under `authoring/penn-station-1935/lighting/phase3/originals/` with its [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license) provenance; it is not the active environment. The earlier Phase 4 visual delivery rebuilt split atlases and static captures. This narrower material/fixture checkpoint retains those exact Phase 4 lighting outputs.

## North-end mural photographic recovery

`assets/mural-north-archival.png` reproduces the actual north-end mural visible in Detroit Publishing Co. negative 071928, [Library of Congress item 2016815386](https://www.loc.gov/item/2016815386/), LC-DIG-det-4a23713. The catalog dates the original photograph approximately 1905–1915 and states **no known restrictions on publication**; it is an original pre-1931 US publication. Credit: **Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Detroit Publishing Company Collection**.

The 728×260 PNG is an exact copy of a mechanically perspective-rectified photographic crop, preserving its original monochrome tones. It is 76,559 bytes; SHA-256 `3fe44b7afcbd89a26bb1db1cdc1108bd525643ae4f556378340168b7afc49076`. Repository-root `authoring/penn-station-1935/references/phase3/mural-north-provenance.json` records the original TIFF URL/hash, image corners and treatment. There is no generated painting, inpainting, invented lettering or colourisation. The global-contrast inspection derivative is research-only and is not bundled.

The original 5076×4061 TIFF was retrieved again and matches its retained SHA-256 `b11e92e022cd1aec75b78c3164f35bc2e7772a336f4d31029e2f2ed9665f5d9d`. Its native mural crop remains 728×260; an enlarged interpolation would not recover additional historical detail.

Only the north-end field may use this image. Its official title and original colours are unverified; low resolution, exposure falloff, dust and photographic defects remain visible. It does not reconstruct the other five murals, which remain neutral. The [interior evidence ledger](model/interior-evidence.md) records the placement reasoning and limitations.

The [1911 US Copyright Office catalog](https://archive.org/details/catalogofcopyrig64libr), printed p. 80, independently records Detroit negative 071928, copyright 16 January 1911, registration J151601, with copies deposited 6 February 1911. This corroborates the original-publication era rather than relying solely on the LOC catalog's broad date range.

No generated imagery, synthetic body or replacement clothing was used. The source file hashes and changes are recorded in the scratch proof's `source-and-review.json`; the source grant and mirror are retained in the research manifest. Any future redistribution must preserve the model title, Renderpeople credit, original item link, CC BY 4.0 link and modification notice. The original creator's NoAI tag is retained in provenance.
