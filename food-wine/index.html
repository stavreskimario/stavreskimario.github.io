    A few little details make a better match.

      The sauce matters, too.

      A lemon dressing, mushroom sauce or tomato base can change the pairing as much as the main ingredient.

      A little pairing know-how

      Match the mood of the meal

      Think about the sauce and the dish’s weight. Fresh acidity can lift richness; a little sweetness can soften chilli heat. For dessert, choose a wine at least as sweet as the food.

      A guide, with room for taste

      Matches use a curated style catalogue and pairing rules. Regions are examples to explore. Budgets are broad editorial estimates in Australian dollars, not retailer quotes or availability checks.
